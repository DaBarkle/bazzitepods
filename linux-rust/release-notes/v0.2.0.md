First BazzitePods release: a zero-config AirPods app for Bazzite and other Flatpak desktops, built on LibrePods.

**Install** (no sudo):
```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/main/linux-rust/packaging/install-bazzite.sh | bash
```

- Accurate battery levels for the left pod, right pod and case, plus charging state
- Game overlay with Compact, Pop-up, Full and Off modes, a liquid-glass look, and translucency over fullscreen games. It's anti-cheat safe: no injection and no screen capture.
- Fixes the "connected but silent after walking away" bug, and reconnects by itself
- Switching between devices: when another device takes the audio the app shows **On another device**, and **Use on this PC** takes it back
- Noise control, ear detection, conversation awareness and renaming
- Starts at login and lives in the tray

The `BazzitePods.flatpak` bundle is attached to this release by CI.
