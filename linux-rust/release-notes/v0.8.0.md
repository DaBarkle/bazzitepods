# Kase 0.8.0

**Released:** 2026-10-29 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none (old installer link, settings and leftovers from older builds are all handled for you)

AirPods for Linux. Kase grew up on Bazzite and KDE Plasma, and still gives its best there, but this release makes it at home on every major distribution and desktop: one installer that knows your distribution, PulseAudio as well as PipeWire, Plasma 5, Sway, Hyprland and niri, and PCs with two Bluetooth adapters. A new Diagnostics section shows at a glance what your system can and can't do.

## Highlights

- **One install command for every distribution.** Fedora, Bazzite, SteamOS, Ubuntu, Debian, Arch, openSUSE and NixOS. If Flatpak is missing, the installer tells you the exact command to get it.
- **More desktops, fewer surprises.** The island turns see-through over fullscreen games on Plasma 5, Sway, Hyprland and niri, not just Plasma 6, and the tray appears the moment your panel does.
- **Finds your AirPods wherever they're paired.** A PC with a built-in chip and a USB dongle now uses the adapter the AirPods actually live on.
- **Diagnostics in Settings.** Adapter, airplane mode, AAC or SBC, how battery is read while the AirPods are away, and a warning for Bluetooth chips known to struggle with AirPods.

## What's new

### Installing

- **`install.sh`** replaces `install-bazzite.sh`; the old link keeps working and runs the new script. It reads `/etc/os-release` and, if Flatpak is missing, prints the right command (`apt`, `dnf`, `pacman`, `zypper`, or NixOS's `services.flatpak.enable = true;`). ([#31])
- **Clear about CPUs.** The installer checks your CPU and stops with a plain message on anything other than x86_64, instead of failing half way. ([#31])

### Bluetooth

- **The right adapter.** Kase searches every adapter for your paired AirPods instead of assuming the default one, and switches when that adapter is plugged in later. ([#32])
- **Apple device mode beyond Fedora.** On NixOS, Settings gives you the `hardware.bluetooth.settings` line to add (NixOS generates `/etc`). Systems without systemd restart Bluetooth through OpenRC or runit. SteamOS explains that the account needs a password first, and a desktop without a password prompt (polkit agent) gets a clear message. The script no longer relies on GNU-only `sed`. ([#32])

### Audio

- **PulseAudio as well as PipeWire.** The audio guard understands PulseAudio's profile names (`a2dp_sink`, `headset_head_unit`), and the log says which sound server is running. ([#33])
- **Knows what's really playing.** JamesDSP, NoiseTorch, loopbacks, filter chains and monitor streams no longer count as music, and Zoom, TeamSpeak, Mumble, Slack, Teams, Element, Signal, Telegram, Skype and Webex count as calls, like Discord. Streams are recognised by their program and node names, not only by the name they show. ([#33])
- **The AirPods mic on newer FFmpeg.** libavcodec 63 is accepted, and both an unknown FFmpeg and a sound server that refuses Kase's mixer rights are logged plainly. ([#33])

### Desktops

- **Fullscreen transparency on Sway, Hyprland and niri**, from the compositor's own window list (wlr foreign-toplevel management), and on Plasma 5 through a KWin script that speaks both Plasma 5 and 6. ([#34])
- **Back at the PC on KDE.** KDE never marks the session idle in logind, so Kase now also listens for the screen saver ending (KDE and GNOME) to bring the AirPods back when you return. ([#34])
- **The tray appears as soon as the panel does,** instead of up to 30 seconds later. GNOME users are told once to turn on the AppIndicator extension. ([#34])
- **Start at login on Sway, Hyprland, niri and COSMIC.** Where the desktop can't register Kase itself, it tells you once, in a notification and the log, the exact line to add. ([#34])
- **The island survives GPU trouble.** If it keeps crashing on start (no Vulkan, or an NVIDIA Flatpak driver that doesn't match), it tries once more drawing in software before falling back to notifications. ([#34])

### Diagnostics

- A new **Diagnostics** section at the bottom of Settings: the Bluetooth adapter in use and whether it's on or blocked, AAC or SBC-only (and why), whether battery-while-away uses the advertisement monitor or fallback scanning, and a warning for Realtek RTL8761B dongles. ([#32])

### Under the hood

- **AirPods for Linux.** The README, app metadata and docs are no longer Bazzite-only: compatibility per desktop, AAC per distribution, troubleshooting for NVIDIA, older GPUs and dual-GPU laptops, and build packages for Fedora, Arch and Ubuntu. ([#31])
- **The last "BazzitePods" names are gone.** The virtual mic is `kase_airpods_mic` and Apple device mode's backup is `main.conf.bak-kase`. Leftovers from older builds are still found and cleaned up, and a DeviceID an older build set aside is still restored. ([#35])
- The installer scripts are checked with shellcheck (`just lint-packaging`). ([#31])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

The old `install-bazzite.sh` command does the same. Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

If you use Apple device mode, it keeps working as it is. Your original backup (`main.conf.bak-bazzitepods`) is left in place, and turning the mode off restores your settings as before.

## Known limitations

- **GNOME:** the island is still shown as desktop notifications; GNOME has no overlay layer for apps.
- **Steam Gaming Mode:** no island (gamescope has no overlay layer for other apps).
- **Sway, Hyprland, niri:** Kase can't add itself to startup there; add the line it shows you once.
- **Ubuntu, Debian, openSUSE:** music plays over SBC, because these distributions ship Bluetooth audio without AAC.
- **ARM:** no 64-bit ARM bundle yet. ARM builds, and shellcheck of the installer in CI, are coming in a follow-up release; until then the installer stops with a clear message on non-x86_64 CPUs.
- The new desktop and distribution paths are covered by unit tests; day-to-day hardware testing is still on Bazzite with KDE Plasma 6 and AirPods 4.
