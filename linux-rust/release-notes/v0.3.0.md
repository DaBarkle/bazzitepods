# BazzitePods 0.3.0

**Released:** 2026-10-27 · **Type:** minor release, no breaking changes

The overlay has been redesigned after the iPhone's Dynamic Island. It opens into a card when your AirPods connect, rests as a slim island, and turns into a short alert that names exactly what just happened: the case started charging, a pod is running low, the noise control mode changed, or your iPhone took the audio. Every change of shape runs on springs, so it moves the way the real island does. It is also lighter on your games than before: it draws only while it moves and uses no CPU at rest.

## Added

- **The island.** The overlay now has three shapes, like the Dynamic Island ([#11](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/11)):
  - a **card** with a ring for the left pod, the right pod and the case, the device name and its status (noise control mode, or where the audio is playing). It opens when the AirPods connect, when a pod comes or goes, and when you press Preview;
  - a **slim island** with all three levels, which stays on screen in Compact mode;
  - **alerts**, a wide pill for one piece of news: *Case charging* (green ring), *Right AirPod low* (amber at 20%, red at 10%), *Playing on iPhone*, *Back on this PC*, and *Disconnected*.
- **Noise control alerts.** Switching between Noise Cancellation, Transparency, Adaptive Audio and Off, on the AirPods or in the app, shows the new mode with its symbol, as an iPhone does. ([#11](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/11))
- **Fluid motion.** The island grows out of a small pill with a slight bounce and folds back without one. Old contents fade out quickly while the new ones fade and scale in. Rings sweep up to their level when a card or alert opens, and a percentage that changes rolls to its new value. In Full mode, news rolls into the card's second line with a small bounce instead of covering the card. If something new happens mid-animation, the island carries its motion on towards the new shape instead of jumping or restarting. ([#11](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/11))

## Changed

- **Top center by default.** New installs place the island at the top center of the screen, where the Dynamic Island sits. If you already picked a position, it is kept; choose **Top center** under Settings → Overlay position for the full effect. ([#11](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/11))
- **Glass at 0% is the island's pure black** (was dark smoked glass). Higher values still clear towards liquid glass, and Game opacity works as before. ([#11](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/11))
- **Fewer, more useful pop-ups.** The overlay alerts when charging starts and when a battery drops to 20% or 10%. It no longer pops up when charging stops or a level climbs back out of the low range. ([#11](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/11))
- **Lighter on games.** The overlay now redraws only while something moves, in step with the compositor's frames, so animations are as smooth as your monitor's refresh rate allows. At rest it draws nothing and sleeps until its next deadline instead of checking five times a second. ([#11](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/11))

### Resource use

Measured with the release build on a test desktop:

| Moment | CPU | Wake-ups per second |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AirPods not connected | 0% | 0 |
| Island at rest (Compact, Pop-up or Full) | 0% | 0 |
| During a transition (about half a second to a second) | draws once per displayed frame, then stops | |

## Known issues

- The case reports its battery only while a pod is in it, as on an iPhone. After both pods come out, the island shows its last known level, dimmed, with how long ago it was seen.
- Same as 0.2.2: BazzitePods can't pause apps that don't report playback to the system (such as the Plex desktop app) when the iPhone takes the AirPods.

## Upgrading

Run the installer again. It replaces the current version and keeps your settings:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/main/linux-rust/packaging/install-bazzite.sh | bash
```

To install by hand instead, download `BazzitePods.flatpak` below and run:

```bash
flatpak install --user --reinstall --bundle BazzitePods.flatpak
```

## Compatibility

- **Desktops:** Bazzite and other Fedora Atomic desktops on KDE Plasma 6 (full support, including the overlay); other Flatpak desktops (battery, controls and tray, with notifications instead of the overlay).
- **AirPods:** AirPods 4 (with and without noise cancellation), AirPods Pro 2 and 3, AirPods 3, AirPods Max and others. Noise control alerts appear on models that have noise control.
- **Anti-cheat:** unchanged. The overlay draws on its own desktop layer, with no injection, no hooking and no screen capture.
