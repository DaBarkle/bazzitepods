# Kase 0.9.1

**Released:** 2026-10-31 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

When your AirPods connect, the sound now always goes to them. This release also delivers everything in [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) (the case card, faster pickup from the case, and staying connected alongside your iPhone) to anyone updating straight from 0.8.

## Fixed

- **The sound follows the AirPods when they connect.** Kase used to make the AirPods the default output only if they had been the default the last time they were connected. It kept that in memory only, so after Kase restarted, or after another output was selected for a moment, the AirPods could connect while the sound stayed on the speakers or HDMI. Now every new connection makes the AirPods the default output and moves what is playing to them. ([#44])
- Kase holds back only when it should, and says why in its log:
  - **You picked another output while the AirPods were connected:** Kase leaves your choice alone until they next connect.
  - **Both pods are in the case:** nothing changes until you take one out, then the sound moves to them.
  - **Another device is playing on the AirPods:** your iPhone keeps them; the sound moves when it lets go.
  - **Your default is a virtual output such as EasyEffects:** it already forwards to the AirPods, so your effects stay in place. ([#44])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

Everything listed for [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) still applies.
