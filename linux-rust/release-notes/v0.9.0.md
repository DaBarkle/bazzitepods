# Kase 0.9.0

**Released:** 2026-10-31 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none

Opening the case now shows what's in it, the AirPods come back from the case faster, and Kase can keep this PC connected while your iPhone shares the AirPods. This release also fixes the Bluetooth advertisement monitor, which stopped right after it started.

## Added

- **A card when you open the case.** When your AirPods connect from the open case, the island shows both pods' and the case's battery for about 6 seconds. It folds away 2 seconds after you take a pod out. Closing the lid with the pods inside no longer pops a "Disconnected" alert, and charging news from pods sitting in the case stays quiet. The card is never shown over a fullscreen game or video. Turn it off with **Show a card when I open the case** in the **Island** tab. ([#43])
- **Stay connected alongside my iPhone.** While your iPhone shares the AirPods, Kase asks them to keep this PC on their list of devices, with whichever device is playing first, so they don't drop the PC for the iPhone. Kase repeats the request only when something changes, at most once every 2 seconds and 8 times per connection, and logs every one. The switch is in **Settings → AirPods** (on by default) and works together with Apple device mode. ([#43])

## Changed

- **Faster pickup from the case.** After the AirPods go into the case, Kase now tries to reconnect every 8 seconds for 15 minutes while you're at the PC (screen unlocked, not idle, nothing fullscreen), so opening the lid connects them within seconds. At other times, and over fullscreen games, the usual schedule applies: every 20 seconds for 3 minutes, then every minute. ([#43])

## Fixed

- **The Bluetooth advertisement monitor no longer stops a few milliseconds after it starts.** With **Quicker battery updates when not connected** on, Kase registered the monitor and then let it go straight away, once a minute, so it never saw the AirPods' adverts. It now stays registered for as long as Kase runs. ([#43])

## Credits

"Stay connected alongside my iPhone" follows an idea and protocol detail from [EarPort](https://github.com/Anoryth/earport) by Anoryth (GPL-3.0), reimplemented for Kase.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact. Both new switches start on.

## Known limitations

- On some AirPods and Bluetooth adapters, no proximity adverts reach the PC at all (seen with MediaTek adapters). Kase then relies on reconnecting, as before.
- Everything listed for [0.8.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.8.0) through [0.8.3](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.8.3) still applies.
