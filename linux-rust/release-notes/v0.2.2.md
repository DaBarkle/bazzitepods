# BazzitePods 0.2.2

**Released:** 2026-10-25 · **Type:** patch release, no breaking changes

Switching your AirPods between an iPhone and your PC now behaves the way it does between Apple devices: the device you last pressed play on gets the AirPods. This works with every media app on the PC, including ones like the Plex desktop app that don't report playback to the system. The overlay now shows where your audio went and when it comes back.

## Added

- **Switch feedback on the overlay.** When your iPhone takes the AirPods, the overlay shows **Playing on iPhone**. When they return, it shows **Back on this PC**. The window's banner names the device too. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))

## Fixed

- **Any app brings the AirPods back.** Start playback in any app, including the Plex desktop app, and the AirPods return from the iPhone within about a second. ([#5](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/5))
- **No more pause right after pressing play.** Pressing play on the PC while the iPhone was still playing could pause the PC again a moment later. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **Sound comes back with the AirPods.** When the AirPods return from the iPhone, sound plays right away. Before, they could connect silently until you changed the output by hand. Voice calls in apps like Discord move back to the AirPods too. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **No sound from the speakers during a switch.** When the AirPods come back, the PC rebuilds their audio output, and for about a second apps used to fall back to your speakers. The speakers are now muted for that moment, so the sound only comes back on the AirPods. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **No tug of war with the iPhone.** While the iPhone is playing, it keeps the AirPods. The PC no longer grabs them back because an app such as Plex keeps its audio open after pausing, because the PC's own sound check notices the output went quiet, or because a browser it just paused briefly reports playing again. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **The AirPods come home when the iPhone stops.** If media is still playing on the PC when you stop playback on the iPhone, the AirPods return to the PC by themselves. This now also works for media that was already playing before the iPhone connected. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **The PC reconnects by itself.** After the AirPods drop the PC, BazzitePods now reconnects them on its own: within about 20 seconds when they made room for an iPhone, so they can come back to the PC when the iPhone is done, and when you take them out of the case again, as a Mac does, however long you were away, even after a reboot. Before, they often stayed off the PC until you connected them by hand. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **Phone calls are never interrupted.** A call on the iPhone now pauses the PC right away, and the PC no longer takes the AirPods back during a call. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **Faster recovery after a notification.** If an iPhone notification briefly takes the AirPods while you're playing on the PC, the PC takes them back as soon as the iPhone lets go. This now works for every app. ([#5](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/5))

## Changed

- **Playback is detected from audio streams.** BazzitePods now also watches the system's audio streams to see when something starts playing. It ignores notification sounds, Discord's idle voice connections, EasyEffects' processing stream and very short sounds. ([#5](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/5), [#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **Correct version number.** The app now reports 0.2.2. Earlier builds reported 0.2.0. Discover and other software centres show the release history. ([#6](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/6))
- **Quieter logs.** A harmless message right after the AirPods disconnect is no longer logged as an error. ([#5](https://github.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/pull/5))

## Known issues

- BazzitePods can't pause apps that don't report playback to the system, such as the Plex desktop app, when the iPhone takes the AirPods. Pause them yourself. Switching back still works.
- Inside the Flatpak sandbox, BazzitePods can't set the default audio output. This normally makes no difference, because the system picks the AirPods by itself when they connect.

## Upgrading

Run the installer again. It replaces the current version and keeps your settings:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/main/linux-rust/packaging/install-bazzite.sh | bash
```

To install by hand instead, download `BazzitePods.flatpak` below and run:

```bash
flatpak install --user --reinstall --bundle BazzitePods.flatpak
```

## Compatibility

- **Desktops:** Bazzite and other Fedora Atomic desktops on KDE Plasma 6 (full support, including the overlay); other Flatpak desktops (battery, controls and tray).
- **AirPods:** AirPods 4, AirPods Pro 2 and 3, AirPods 3, AirPods Max and others.
- **Anti-cheat:** unchanged. The overlay draws on its own desktop layer, with no injection, no hooking and no screen capture.
