# Kase 0.9.3

**Released:** 2026-10-31 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

When you press Disconnect on the PC, the AirPods now stay disconnected until you connect them again, even after Kase restarts, the PC reboots or it wakes from sleep.

## Fixed

- **Disconnect is remembered.** Kase used to keep a Disconnect pressed on the PC in memory only. After a restart, a reboot or an update, it reconnected the AirPods like any other AirPods that had wandered off. Kase now saves the Disconnect for each pair of AirPods. While it's saved, Kase doesn't try to reconnect them at all: no timed attempts, nothing when you open the case lid and no "Stay connected alongside my iPhone". ([#46])
- **Switching Bluetooth off isn't mistaken for a Disconnect.** Linux reports both the same way, so Kase waits a few seconds and saves the Disconnect only if Bluetooth is still on. ([#46])

Every decision is logged, for example `remembering the Disconnect`, `remembered Disconnect pressed on this PC; not reconnecting` and `connected again; forgetting the Disconnect pressed on this PC`.

## How it ends

Connect the AirPods again any way you like and Kase looks after them as before:

- from your desktop's Bluetooth settings or `bluetoothctl`,
- or by the AirPods connecting to the PC by themselves.

Kase never blocks a connection. If the AirPods connect while a Disconnect is saved, Kase treats that as your choice and forgets it. Unpairing the AirPods also clears it, even if you do it while Kase isn't running.

## Changed

- **The README is up to date** with everything from 0.9.0 to 0.9.3: the case card and its setting, faster pickup from the case, opening the lid to connect, Stay connected alongside my iPhone (with credit to EarPort), when the sound moves to the AirPods, and remembered Disconnects. There is a new FAQ entry for AirPods that don't reconnect by themselves. ([#46])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- If Kase stops within a few seconds of a Disconnect (you quit it, or log out straight away), the Disconnect may not be saved yet.
- When your iPhone takes the AirPods first, the PC still has to wait for the iPhone to let go.
- Everything listed for [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) still applies.
