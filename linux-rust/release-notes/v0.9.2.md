# Kase 0.9.2

**Released:** 2026-10-31 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

Reconnecting from the case is faster and more dependable, especially when you switch between your iPhone and the PC. Kase now uses the AirPods' own Bluetooth adverts, which it can hear reliably since the advertisement monitor fix in 0.9.0.

## Fixed

- **Quick case reopens after using your iPhone now reconnect quickly.** Kase learned that the pods were in the case only from the AirPods' ear reports. After the iPhone hands the AirPods back, they sometimes send the PC no ear reports at all, so putting them in the case counted as "left this PC" and the slow schedule applied. The AirPods' adverts now count too, and whichever is newest wins. ([#45])
- **Opening the lid connects at once.** When an advert shows the case lid opening, Kase tries to connect straight away instead of waiting up to 10 seconds. Attempts still stay at least 5 seconds apart, so it never floods the radio. ([#45])
- **An iPhone that grabs the AirPods mid-connect no longer slows the PC down.** If the AirPods drop the PC within 10 seconds of connecting (usually because the iPhone took them first), Kase keeps trying every 8 seconds for the next 3 minutes instead of every 20, and connects as soon as the iPhone lets go. As with the case retries, this only happens while you are at the PC and nothing is fullscreen. ([#45])

Every one of these decisions is logged, for example `AirPods … are in the case (from adverts)`, `case lid opened; trying to connect now` and `dropped this PC while connecting (another device took them)`.

## Added

- **A case and reconnect scenario table** in `docs/TESTING.md`. It lists what Kase does when you reopen the case quickly or slowly, when the iPhone grabs the AirPods, with one pod in the case, with no adverts, when locked, idle or gaming, after suspend, after a Disconnect, and out of range, plus the code that handles each. ([#45])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- When your iPhone takes the AirPods first, the PC still has to wait for the iPhone to let go. Kase can only connect sooner.
- A Disconnect pressed on the PC is forgotten if Kase restarts before the AirPods come back.
- Everything listed for [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) still applies.
