# BazzitePods 0.4.0

**Released:** 2026-10-27 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none

This is the first published release since 0.2.2. It rolls up versions 0.3.0, 0.3.1 and 0.3.2, which were never released separately, and the new 0.4.0 work.

## Highlights

- **A new overlay, modelled on the iPhone's Dynamic Island.** It uses 0% CPU while idle.
- **AirPods reconnect after a long time away.** Before, they could stay disconnected after a workday.
- **The AirPods can be the PC's microphone while music stays in full quality.** This is opt-in.
- **More honest battery readings and five more AirPods settings.**

## Added

- **AirPods mic switch (opt-in).** Turn on *AirPods mic switch* in More options, then hold the left stem to swap between your desk mic and the AirPods mic. Music keeps playing in full quality. BazzitePods does not drop to the Bluetooth headset profile; it uses the separate mic stream the AirPods use with Apple devices. While the AirPods mic is selected it is the system default, and apps that were set to the desk mic, such as Discord, are moved across and back. The window and tray also have a *Use AirPods mic* switch. ([#15])
- **Right stem hold changes noise control** while the mic switch is on. It cycles through the noise control modes enabled on the AirPods, on models with noise cancellation. ([#15])
- **The island overlay.** The overlay now takes three forms:
  - A card with a ring each for the left pod, the right pod and the case.
  - A slim island that shows all three levels.
  - Short alerts for charging, low battery (20% and 10%), noise control changes, a hand-off to or from an iPhone, a disconnect, and which mic is in use.
  
  Transitions use spring animation. ([#11], [#15])
- **More AirPods settings.** Automatic ear detection, Adaptive Audio strength, noise cancellation with one AirPod, microphone choice (Automatic, Left or Right) and *Connect to this PC automatically*. Each appears only when your AirPods report it. ([#12])
- **"In case" status.** With both pods in a closed case, the window and tray say *In case* instead of *Not connected*, and a hint says to open the lid to update the case reading. ([#12])

## Fixed

- **AirPods that went out of range never reconnected after a long absence.** Retries only ran while the PC recognised the AirPods' Bluetooth adverts, and they stopped after 4 hours. Every drop now gets the same schedule as putting the AirPods in the case: every 20 s, then every minute, then every 3 min, until they are back. ([#16])
- **Disconnecting on this PC is respected.** BazzitePods now reads why BlueZ dropped the link. AirPods you disconnect from the KDE Bluetooth menu stay disconnected, and a drop caused by distance always retries. ([#16])
- **Levels and charging were greyed out while the AirPods were away from this PC.** Readings the AirPods broadcast nearby now show live, including charging. ([#12])
- **Stale levels looked live for up to a minute after a disconnect.** They now switch to *last seen* straight away. ([#12])
- **Personalized Volume showed Off after every launch,** whatever the AirPods were set to. ([#12])

## Changed

- **The overlay sits at the top center by default.** This applies to new installs only. A position you already chose is kept. ([#11])
- **Glass at 0% is now pure black,** matching the Dynamic Island. Before, it was smoked glass. ([#11])
- **Fewer pop-ups.** Alerts appear when charging starts and when a level drops to 20% or 10%. They no longer appear when charging stops or a level recovers. ([#11])
- **The overlay redraws only while animating,** in step with the compositor. When idle it uses 0% CPU and has no timer wake-ups. ([#11])
- **Stem control settings apply immediately.** Before, they took effect on the next reconnect. ([#15])
- **Build: faster CI, GitHub Actions updated to current versions, and a warning-free build.** None of this changes how the app behaves. ([#14])

## Known issues

- **The mic switch has not yet been used on real hardware as a full build.** The AirPods mic stream was confirmed on AirPods 4 (ANC), and the switch logic is covered by automated tests, which is why it ships off by default. If the AirPods stop sending mic audio for 3 s, BazzitePods switches back to the desk mic and shows *AirPods Mic Unavailable*.
- **Music quality drops slightly while the AirPods mic is in use.** This happens inside the AirPods; the PC still sends full quality. For that reason the mic stream runs only while the AirPods mic is selected.
- **Stem holds are claimed by BazzitePods while the mic switch is on.** A hold won't change noise control while the AirPods are on an iPhone.
- **The case battery is reported only while the lid is open with a pod inside,** the same as on an iPhone. At other times the last reading is shown along with its age.
- **Apps that don't report playback to the system can't be paused on hand-off to an iPhone.** The Plex desktop app is one example.

## Upgrading

Run the installer again. It replaces the installed version and keeps your settings:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/main/linux-rust/packaging/install-bazzite.sh | bash
```

Or download `BazzitePods.flatpak` below and run:

```bash
flatpak install --user --reinstall --bundle BazzitePods.flatpak
```

If you set the overlay position yourself, consider switching it to the top-center spot under **Overlay position** in Settings.

## Compatibility

- **Desktops:**
  - Bazzite and other Fedora Atomic desktops on KDE Plasma 6 get full support, including the overlay.
  - Other Flatpak desktops get battery, controls and the tray icon, with notifications instead of the overlay.
- **AirPods:**
  - AirPods 4 (with and without ANC), AirPods Pro 2 and 3, AirPods 3, AirPods Max and others are supported.
  - Noise control features need a model with noise control.
- **Anti-cheat:** unchanged. BazzitePods never injects into or hooks game processes and never captures the screen. The overlay is a separate desktop layer.
