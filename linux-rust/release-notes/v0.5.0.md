# BazzitePods 0.5.0

**Released:** 2026-10-28 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none

This release redesigns the app window and adds a second look for the island overlay, *Purist*, which follows the iPhone's Dynamic Island closely: solid black, a hairline edge, and light that runs round the border when something happens. It adds island sizes for large or high-resolution screens, turns Apple device mode into a one-click switch, and makes the AirPods mic swap work properly on AirPods 4, with a chime so you know which mic you're on. It also makes switching mics and reconnecting more reliable: music stays on full-quality AAC, never jumps to the speakers mid-swap, and battery status recovers on its own if the AirPods' control connection stalls. The mic fixes planned for 0.4.1 are included here; there is no separate 0.4.1 release.

## Highlights

- **Purist island style, now the default.** Flat black, with event-driven light round the border.
- **Island sizes.** Small, Medium, Large and Extra large.
- **A redesigned app window** in the same design language as the island.
- **One-click Apple device mode.** No terminal: flip a switch, enter your password, done.
- **The AirPods mic swap works on AirPods 4,** moves every app (Discord included), and chimes when it switches.
- **More reliable mic swaps and reconnects.** No sound on the speakers, no getting stuck on a lower-quality codec, and battery status that heals itself.
- **Still costs nothing at rest.** Every animation ends within two seconds and the overlay then draws no frames.

## Added

- **Purist island style.** The new default look. The island is opaque black with a hairline edge tinted to match the alert, the way Apple draws the Dynamic Island. When something happens, light runs round the border in that event's colour ([#20]):
  - **Charging:** two green lights run from the top edge round both sides and meet underneath.
  - **Low battery:** the border breathes twice, amber at 20% and red at 10%.
  - **Audio moves to or from another device:** a blue sweep.
  - **Noise control changes:** a quick flash with a ripple (blue for noise cancellation, a colour spectrum for Adaptive).
  - **AirPods mic selected:** an orange flash, the colour iOS uses for a mic in use.
  - **AirPods connect:** a flowing colour spectrum, in the style of Apple Intelligence.
- **Island size.** Choose **S**, **M**, **L** or **XL** under **Size** in the Island tab to scale the whole island (0.85x to 1.45x). ([#20])
- **A sound when the mic swaps.** Holding the left stem plays a soft rising chime when the AirPods mic turns on and a falling one when the desk mic comes back. ([#21])
- **Liquid motion in the purist style.** The island gives a quick head shake when the AirPods mic can't be used, and its outline gives a little height as it stretches, like a drop of liquid. ([#20])

## Changed

- **Purist is the default island style.** Choose *Classic* under **Style** in the Island tab for the previous glass look. ([#20])
- **Redesigned app window.** Pure black like the island, three tabs (AirPods, Island, Settings), a glowing status island, battery rings and selections that move on springs, and a preview screen for placing the overlay. Island style and size live in the Island tab under Look. ([#19])
- **The purist island uses the bundled Inter font,** the same one as the app window.
- **Apple device mode is one switch.** Flip it in the Settings tab, confirm, and enter your password in the system prompt. BazzitePods makes the change, restarts Bluetooth and shows when it's on. Turning it off restores your original Bluetooth settings. If your system can't show a password prompt (no `pkexec`), the app still offers the command to copy. ([#18])

## Fixed

- **The left stem hold swaps the mic on AirPods 4.** The AirPods ignored the request to hand stem holds to the PC when it was sent during connection, so holding the stem changed noise control instead. BazzitePods now resends it once the AirPods settle, after taking them back from another device, and whenever the AirPods reset it. ([#21])
- **The AirPods mic sends sound.** Its audio wasn't decoded with the FFmpeg inside the Flatpak, so apps recorded silence. ([#21])
- **The swap works inside the Flatpak.** The sound server refused to let BazzitePods change the default mic or move apps between mics; it now identifies itself as an audio manager, as EasyEffects and Discord do. This also fixes moving music back to the AirPods after a reconnect. ([#21])
- **Discord moves to the AirPods mic.** Apps set to a mic by name, or recording from EasyEffects' mic, now move to the AirPods mic and back to the mic they were using. ([#17])
- **Apps that open the mic after the swap move too,** such as Discord when you join a voice channel, within two seconds. ([#17])
- An app that keeps returning to the desk mic is left there instead of being moved back and forth every two seconds. ([#21])
- **Swapping back to the desk mic keeps your sound off the speakers.** The AirPods drop their music connection when their mic stops. The speakers are now muted before that happens and stay muted until the AirPods play again, and if they don't, BazzitePods repairs the connection at once instead of after several seconds of silence. ([#22])
- **The switch chime plays even when system notification sounds are muted.** ([#22])
- **Battery and connection status keep updating after a reconnect.** The AirPods' control connection could stop responding after a reconnect while music kept playing, leaving the app showing them disconnected with no battery. BazzitePods now detects a stalled control connection and reopens it by itself within about 20 seconds. ([#22])
- **Music no longer gets stuck on a lower-quality codec.** If the music stream doesn't restart after a mic swap (PipeWire briefly locks a Bluetooth stream out after an error, and repeating the reset only extended that), BazzitePods now switches codec once to get the sound back within about two seconds instead of seven, then returns to AAC as soon as nothing is playing, including after a reconnect. ([#22])

## Known issues

- While the AirPods mic is in use, apps that were on EasyEffects' mic bypass EasyEffects (it keeps processing the desk mic). The AirPods do their own noise reduction.
- On a computer where the GPU renderer isn't available and the overlay falls back to software drawing, the purist style shows a plain black island without the border light.
- At Extra large on a small screen, the full card can be wide; Large suits most 1440p screens.

## Upgrading

Run the installer again. It replaces the installed version and keeps your settings:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/bazzitepods/main/linux-rust/packaging/install-bazzite.sh | bash
```

Or download `BazzitePods.flatpak` below and run:

```bash
flatpak install --user --reinstall --bundle BazzitePods.flatpak
```

The island switches to the new *Purist* style at *Medium* size. To keep the glass look, choose *Classic* under **Style** in the Island tab; it looks exactly as before. If you had already picked a style, your choice is kept.

## Compatibility

- **Desktops:**
  - Bazzite and other Fedora Atomic desktops on KDE Plasma 6 get full support, including the overlay.
  - Other Flatpak desktops get battery, controls and the tray icon, with notifications instead of the overlay.
- **AirPods:** unchanged from 0.4.0. The mic swap is confirmed on AirPods 4 (ANC).
- **Anti-cheat:** unchanged. The overlay is a separate desktop layer drawn by its own process. It never injects into or hooks game processes and never captures the screen. The new shader runs only on the overlay's own surface.
