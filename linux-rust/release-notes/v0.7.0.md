# Kase 0.7.0

**Released:** 2026-10-29 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none (settings and saved AirPods move across by themselves)

Your PC is home. This release makes switching between your iPhone and your PC something you never have to think about: the AirPods reconnect after a day away, a phone notification no longer keeps them while you game, and they come back to the PC by themselves when your iPhone stops playing. It also brings ten fixes and a leaner codebase from a full review of Kase.

## Highlights

- **The AirPods come back after a day away.** Open the case when you get home, or just unlock your PC, and they reconnect. No more trips to the Bluetooth menu or restarting Kase.
- **Notifications don't steal your game audio.** A ding on your phone only borrows the AirPods; the sound is back on the PC about 3 seconds later.
- **Your PC is home.** A few seconds after your iPhone stops playing, the AirPods return to the PC, even if you're in another room.

## Fixed

- **Reconnecting after a day away.** When your iPhone took the AirPods (say, as you left for work), Kase tried to get them back for only 3 minutes and gave up entirely after 4 hours. It now keeps trying, every 20 seconds at first and then every minute, for as long as they're away. Only pressing Disconnect on the PC keeps them away. ([#30])
- **Notifications while gaming.** Kase used to take the AirPods back only when something *new* started playing on the PC. A game that was already running never counted, so the audio stayed on the phone. Now any sound on the PC (games, players, browsers, the Plex app) or a Discord voice channel brings them back about 3 seconds after a notification borrowed them. ([#30])
- **No tug of war.** If you move the AirPods back to your iPhone, or the two devices keep swapping, Kase stops taking them back for two minutes. ([#30])
- **Quitting puts your audio back.** Tray Quit and logging out restore the desk mic, a volume lowered by a conversation, and speakers muted mid-repair. After a crash, the next start unmutes anything left muted. ([#24])
- **Settings survive a power cut.** Saved files are flushed to disk before they replace the old ones. ([#24])
- **The island matches the Settings page.** On a fresh install it drew at full opacity over games while the slider showed 60%; it now follows your settings live. ([#24])
- **Disconnecting from KDE stays disconnected,** even after Bluetooth or D-Bus restarts. ([#24])
- **Audio keeps recovering** when the AirPods' sound card drops out several times in a row, and two audio repairs no longer run at once. ([#24])
- **The case no longer shows 0%** after an out-of-range reading, an unknown ear reading no longer pauses your music, and a dead connection now reconnects. ([#24])
- **Conversation volume** no longer silences the AirPods when it can't read the volume, and is restored if the link drops mid-conversation. ([#24])
- **Island crashes** (for example, no Vulkan) now fall back to notifications instead of restarting forever. ([#24])

## Added

- **Bring AirPods back to this PC** (Settings, on by default). A few seconds after your iPhone stops playing, the AirPods come back to the PC by themselves. Turn it off if you'd rather they stay on the iPhone until you press play on the PC, as with a Mac. A phone call is never interrupted either way. ([#30])
- **Reconnect the moment you're back.** Unlocking the PC, or using it after it went idle, looks for the AirPods straight away. ([#30])
- **A log file** at `~/.var/app/io.github.dabarkle.Kase/.local/state/kase/kase.log`. Every decision about who gets the AirPods is one line with its reason, so a switching problem can be explained after the fact. ([#30])

## Changed

- **A leaner codebase.** Everything Kase doesn't use from LibrePods is gone: the Android app, the old C++ Linux app, the Magisk module, Nix files and Nothing Ear support. The Rust code is now a library with smaller, focused modules, with more tests. ([#25], [#26])
- **The programs are now `kase` and `kase-overlay`,** and their data and config folders are `kase`. Your settings and saved AirPods move across by themselves on first start. ([#27])
- **Releases publish themselves** when a version bump merges, with the Flatpak bundle attached. ([#29])

## Upgrading

Run the usual install command in a terminal:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install-bazzite.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Notes

- Switching was tested with unit tests covering every rule. Real-world testing with an iPhone is the checklist in `docs/TESTING.md`, section 6.
- If a switch ever surprises you, the `route:` lines in the log say what Kase decided and why. Attach the log to an issue.
