# Kase 0.9.4

**Released:** 2026-10-31 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

When the AirPods come back to the PC while your iPhone still has them, pressing play on the PC now takes them back, instead of leaving Discord, videos and Plex silent.

## Fixed

- **Press play and the AirPods come back from the iPhone.** When the AirPods reconnect to the PC while the iPhone is using them, they don't always tell Kase whether they're in your ears. Kase used to read that as "not worn", so pressing play on the PC left them on the iPhone. The PC's sound then went to AirPods that weren't listening: Discord was silent and Plex and browser videos hung. Kase now treats "not said yet" as maybe worn, so pressing play brings them back straight away. Once the AirPods say they're out of your ears or in the case, Kase leaves them alone as before. ([#47])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- When your iPhone takes the AirPods first, the PC still has to wait for the iPhone to let go.
- Everything listed for [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) still applies.
